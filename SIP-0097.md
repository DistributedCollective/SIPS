# SIP-0097: Temporary Pause of the AMM and Zero

## Summary

SIP-0097 proposes a temporary pause of AMM swaps, liquidity deposits and withdrawals, and all delegated Zero operations. Following the lending and AMM security incidents, the pause creates a controlled period to fix vulnerabilities, reconcile affected accounting and complete a broader security review before reopening.

The proposal uses three GovernorOwner ballots: three AMM actions, ten Zero financial/token/custody actions, and six remaining Zero actions. Each executes atomically; all three are required for the complete combined pause. The AMM ballot is independently executable; Zero's full halt requires two ballots to respect the governor's ten-action limit. Existing lending pauses, the XUSD/WRBTC delisting and the empty FeeSharingCollector converter whitelist remain in place.

## Proposed changes

### AMM

Point the main ContractRegistry's `SovrynSwapNetwork`, `SovrynSwapFormula` and `SovrynSwapConverterRegistryData` entries to a dedicated `AMMEmergencyHalt` contract that rejects all calls. The intended effect is to stop swaps, liquidity deposits and withdrawals, including direct converter calls, and block normal converter-catalogue access through the registry.

The proposal does not change pool ownership, reserves, LP balances, AMM SwapSettings or the fee controller. The FeeSharingCollector converter whitelist must remain empty throughout the pause; this is the existing fee-collection barrier, maintained separately by the Exchequer.

### Zero

Install one storage-free `ZeroEmergencyHalt` implementation behind Zero's sixteen existing proxies. It rejects every delegated call, including financial operations, token operations and delegated queries. The proxies retain their native ownership and upgrade methods, allowing later governed restoration. The upgrade does not reset stored balances, debt, collateral, allowances or position records.

Part 1 applies the three AMM changes. Part 2 covers the ten Zero financial, token and custody interfaces; Part 3 halts the six remaining Zero interfaces. The exact targets, order and arguments appear in [Executable actions](#executable-actions) below.

| Executable proposal | Actions | Scope                                                  |
| ------------------- | ------: | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Part 1              |       3 | AMM Network, Formula and ConverterRegistryData entries |
| Part 2              |      10 | Zero financial interfaces, tokens and custody          |
| Part 3              |       6 | Remaining Zero interfaces                              |


## Consequences

The intended AMM pause stops swaps, on-AMM arbitrage, and liquidity additions and redemptions. LP tokens remain transferable, but cannot be redeemed through paused pools. Bots and integrations using these operations may fail.

The Zero halt also stops repayments, collateral top-ups, position closures, redemptions, liquidations, Stability Pool activity, affected token transfers and approvals, and delegated queries. Borrowers lose their normal ways to improve positions; integrations relying on ZUSD or Zero interfaces may also fail. This precautionary pause does not assert that Zero was exploited.

Market prices and time continue moving. Interest, maturities and other time-dependent accounting where applicable can create additional risk and a backlog at reopening.

## Restoration

Reopening requires reviewed fixes, security checks, valid prices and reconciled accounting, followed by separately authorized configuration and governance actions. XUSD/WRBTC stays delisted until its pool consistency is resolved. Converters return to the FeeSharingCollector whitelist only after their fee paths are validated.

This SIP does not approve automatic restoration of old implementations, asset redistribution, debt cancellation, compensation or a reopening date.

## Executable actions

This section defines three independent GovernorOwner proposals. Each action has zero RBTC value. Part 1 pauses the AMM; Parts 2 and 3 complete Zero's sixteen-proxy halt. All three are required for the full combined scope; no cross-proposal atomicity is implied.
